# 🧠 1. **MSSubClass (Building Class)**

👉 This is NOT just a number — it’s a **code describing the type + style + age of house**

### 🔍 What it really represents

It combines:

* number of floors
* age (old/new)
* design type

---

### 🧠 Example thinking

* **20 → 1-story newer house**
  → modern, simple, often suburban

* **30 → 1-story older house**
  → smaller, older construction

* **60 → 2-story newer house**
  → bigger, modern, usually higher value

* **90 → Duplex**
  → 2 families → rental potential

---

### ⚠️ Important

👉 Numbers **don’t mean order**

```text
20 is NOT less than 60 logically
```

👉 It’s just a **category code**

---

# 🧠 2. **MSZoning (Zoning Type)**

👉 This tells **what kind of area the house is legally allowed to be in**

---

### 🔍 Real-world meaning

Government defines zones:

* **RL (Residential Low Density)**
  → big houses, fewer buildings → premium areas

* **RM (Residential Medium Density)**
  → apartments, smaller houses

* **RH (High Density)**
  → crowded areas

* **FV (Floating Village)**
  → modern planned communities

* **C (Commercial)**
  → shops/offices nearby

---

### 🧠 Why it matters

Think:

```text
quiet villa area vs busy commercial street
```

👉 Huge price difference

---

---

### 🧠 Values explained

* **Artery** → near main road (traffic)

* Highway 🚗 → noise → lower
* Railway 🚂 → disturbance
